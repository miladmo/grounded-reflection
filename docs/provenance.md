# Research provenance

This index retains access to the original study record while the [methods](methods.md) and pilot guides provide the public reading path. Protocols, amendments, reviews, incidents and result files remain at their existing paths. Their original dates and wording are preserved.

The historical records mix scientific decisions with operational permissions to an AI-assisted development workflow. Named approvals and quoted execution instructions document who authorised implementation or model calls. They are **not independent peer review, ethics approval or external preregistration**. Material reviews included AI-assisted verification; the author reviewed decisive failure attributions. Scientific *binding approvals* inside the synthetic tasks are different: they define which work records count as evidence.

## Reading historical labels

`LIVE_FREIGABE.md` means “live-run approval”. “Hauptlauf live freigegeben” means “main live run approved”. These labels identify execution permissions, not scientific validation. Original filenames and recorded quotations are retained for traceability.

The [reproducibility guide](reproducibility.md#verification-boundary) describes missing raw records and publication changes. The public repository preserves selected evidence, but it does not provide unrestricted independent reanalysis of the original model runs.
