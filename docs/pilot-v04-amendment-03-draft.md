Each approved field observation constrains one context in one version. Broader consequences must follow from the public hypothesis class. A preference, rejection, unreviewed field or unauthorised revision supplies no approved assignment under this deliberately limited synthetic convention. This limitation remains explicit. The pilot does not yet learn how much informal feedback should count as evidence in real organisations.

## A genuinely structured hypothesis class

The current four independently assigned binary cells permit all sixteen Boolean functions. Saying that a requirement depends on neither, one or both dimensions does not narrow this class. With all sixteen functions, an unseen cell remains free regardless of evidence elsewhere.

Use two binary contextual predicates, x and y, and two field configurations. Zero denotes the configured baseline and one the available alternative. In each registered version, the permitted requirement is one of fourteen distinct functions.

* Always baseline or always alternative.
* One literal, x, not x, y or not y.
* A conjunction of one literal from each dimension.
* A disjunction of one literal from each dimension.

Deduplicate identical truth tables. This includes conditions on neither, one or both dimensions, with unknown polarity. It excludes only XOR and XNOR, the two checkerboard patterns. Publish the complete class definition in the shared task semantics received by all arms. Do not expose the selected world policy, intended regime or evaluator candidate list.

This class is a restricted synthetic model. Its exclusions are not established facts about enterprise requirements. A test of reliable inference under these exclusions must not be presented as unrestricted discovery of latent requirements. Fully crossed evidence outside the class is a material inconsistency and stops generation. No shortest-rule preference, majority vote or hidden prior selects among compatible hypotheses.

The neutral version contract declares one class member per registered version. A review constrains only the member indexed by its recorded version and valid authority interval. No cross-version coupling is part of the class. Keep this public structural assumption even after removing the instructional warning about old evidence. Time validity and provenance therefore remain interpretation tasks under declared semantics, not discoveries of those semantics.

Why this class is preferred for this pilot

* A monotone six-function class would additionally disclose the polarity of nominal context dimensions.
* A conjunction-only class would exclude alternative sufficient triggers.
* Fourteen functions retain both forms and unknown polarity while requiring minimal changes to the existing two-dimensional contracts.

In this class, a new cell can become identifiable from three distinct observed cells. That gain comes from excluding the checkerboard alternatives. It must be named in the report, rather than attributed solely to model reasoning.

## Identical task mix in every setting

Keep the approved two changes, one resolved retention and one unidentifiable change. Split the two changes prospectively into a transfer case and an observed-context case. Thus each setting has exactly the same four types.

The following are hand-checkable construction templates. They use abstract predicates, not model-visible case labels. The future task is a new work item even where its context was observed previously.

| Type | Current authorised observations | Diagnostic context | Oracle conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Change with transfer | 00 gives 0, 01 gives 1, 10 gives 1 | Unobserved 11 | Only x OR y remains. Apply the alternative. |
| Change in an observed context | 00 gives 0, 10 gives 1, one independent repeat | Observed 10 | x, x AND not y, and x OR y remain. All justify change at 10, but do not establish its full scope. |
| Resolved retention with transfer | 00 gives 1, 01 gives 0, 10 gives 0 | Unobserved 11 | Only not x AND not y remains. Retain the baseline. |
| Unidentifiable change | 00 gives 0, 11 gives 1, one independent repeat | Unobserved 10 | x, y, x AND y, and x OR y remain. They disagree at 10. Retain without claiming the baseline is established. |

The last template contains positive evidence for rival contextual explanations. The diagnostic combination distinguishes them. For example, a true world of x makes the diagnostic change necessary but not inferable. At the other unobserved combination 01, that world retains the baseline while the explanation y would change it. Use 01 as its control and report that its retention basis is unresolved, not resolved.

The first three templates have controls in an observed baseline context. Record control basis separately. Do not count retention of an unresolved baseline as proof of correct requirement identification.

All cases have three current approval records. Transfer and resolved-retention cases cover three distinct combinations. Observed-context and unidentifiable cases cover two, with one independent repetition. Apply dimension swaps and value relabellings prospectively. Vary the substantive field-operation direction independently. Do not complement labels in a way that changes a slot's registered regime.

The generator selects true worlds from the public class subject to the prospectively specified slot constraints. The oracle never uses that private sampling plan to remove publicly compatible explanations. Generation failures are recorded and stop the process, rather than triggering seed search.

Six records remain feasible for the small settings.

* S0 and S1 have one registration, three current approval records and two ancillary records.
* S3 has one registration, three current approval records and two forwards.
* S4 has two registrations, one old approval and three current approval records.
* S2 and S5 contain sixty records. Preserve the corresponding evidence core and fill the registered remaining slots prospectively.

Freeze the additional-record quotas as follows. Vary content, context, names and field direction prospectively. Do not sample different mixtures across histories of the same setting.

S5 forwards split equally between current and old origins. A rejected artifact must not contain an authoritative negative verdict on the target field that would itself constrain the requirement. Use whole-artifact rejection for another defect or with unresolved grounds. Ignoring informative negative evidence would be an oracle error, not a legitimate distractor. Audit each added record for accidental decisive information and latent-world contradiction. The new review export must display the counts and example renderings before material approval.

Copied records must not increase the public constraint set. Temporal evidence must refer to the correct version and field. Where current evidence already uniquely determines the policy, copies and old reviews test resistance to misleading apparent support, not additional identification. Do not describe S3 as measuring a statistical benefit of independent samples or S4 as learning change-point detection. The setting comparison remains descriptive, with four diagnostics per setting.

## Oracle and measurement changes

Keep the independent public-evidence oracle and all three truth objects. Replace enumeration of all sixteen functions with enumeration of the declared fourteen. Do not supply the generator's world or intended label to the oracle. Check compatibility and the intended regime independently after generation.

The primary outcome, fixed denominators, conservative error taxonomy, headroom criterion and candidate-error secondary outcome remain unchanged. Report the four task types separately. The new measurement distinction is supported use in an observed context versus warranted transfer to an unobserved combination.

Add deterministic preparation-level scope coverage across the four registered current-version combinations. Compare the combined effect of retained adopted guidance against all oracle-supported changes and against cells where a change is unsupported. This exposes missing warranted scope as well as excessive scope without extra model calls. Score equivalent decompositions by their combined consequences. Do not call an individually narrow valid rule erroneous simply because another candidate covers the remaining scope. Missing preparations and undefined coverage remain explicit.

Keep C1 and C2 candidate errors separate, independent of final correctness. Candidate evidence claims require a separate check from correct rule consequences. Correctly rejected and unresolved candidates are not adoption errors. No new secondary measure enters the headroom criterion.
