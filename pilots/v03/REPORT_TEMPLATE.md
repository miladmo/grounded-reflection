## Interpretation and limits

Give the strongest claim the evidence supports, including null or negative results.
Four constructed pairs cannot establish enterprise ROI, generalisation to new domains
or recursive self-improvement. Note the bounded policy class, selection by calibration,
single generator model and absence of real tool execution. A rating ceiling remains a
measurement limitation, not evidence that methods are equivalent.
